# Non-Coding Tasks

Work a non-technical teammate can own, roughly in the order it's needed.

## Hour 0 to 1: research and story

- **Confirm the survival statistic.** Find a trusted source (American Heart Association, a medical journal) for how much each minute of delay lowers cardiac arrest survival. Save the link for the pitch.
- **Look up SF's real numbers.** SF's ambulance response-time targets and any recent news about slow responses. This sets up the stakes, for example: "SF missed its target X% of the time last year."
- **Name the "what's next" partners.** Who would actually use this: SF's Department of Emergency Management, private ambulance companies. One sentence each.
- **Check the gallery for overlap.** Skim the 93 projects in the [MongoDB SFTW gallery](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/mongodb-sftw-hackathon/hackathon/gallery) and confirm nobody did emergency dispatch, so the pitch can claim it.

## Hour 1 to 3: demo prep

- **Pick the demo cameras.** Browse the Caltrans camera feeds (viewable in a browser) and note which SF cameras show the most traffic. Pick 2 or 3 for the demo.
- **Watch traffic at rush hour.** Around 4:30 to 6pm, watch the chosen cameras and tell B when a real jam appears so it can be recorded. If the hackathon ends before rush hour, flag that early.
- **Choose the demo Tuesday.** Once A has the data, help pick a day from the frozen week with an interesting story, such as a busy evening.
- **Collect the agent's notes.** Save the best plain-language diagnoses the AI writes as they come in, and pick the one to read aloud.

## Hour 3 to 5: the pitch

- **Write and own the pitch script.** Start from `OVERVIEW.md` and fill in the real numbers as they arrive. Time it to the limit.
- **Make the slides.** Title, problem, how it works, result, what's next. Four or five slides max, since the live demo carries the rest.
- **Rehearse and present.** They can present while the builders drive the demo.
- **Record the backup video.** Screen-record a clean run in case the live demo fails on stage.
- **Fill in the submission form.** Project description, team, links, video upload.
- **Test as a judge would.** Watch a run-through and point out anything confusing. If they don't understand it, judges won't either.

## The single most valuable task

Own the pitch from minute one. Builders always run out of time for it, and it's what judges remember.
