# Kinship

## The problem

Scammers can now copy anyone's voice from a few seconds of audio. They call a grandparent, sound exactly like a grandchild, and say they're in trouble and need money fast. The voice is perfect, the panic feels real, and families lose thousands of dollars.

You can't trust your ears anymore. Kinship gives families something better to trust.

## The idea

Kinship turns your family's phones into a secret handshake that can't be faked.

1. **Pair once, in person.** When family members are together, they hold their phones close (or scan a code). That's all it takes. Each phone now quietly recognizes the other.
2. **Verify any call in one tap.** When a call feels off, you tap **Verify** in Kinship. The real person's phone gets a request and they confirm it with their face or fingerprint.
3. **Get a clear answer.** Your screen turns **green** if it's really them. If nobody can answer, or the answer is fake, it turns **red**.

A scammer can copy a voice. They can't copy the phone in your grandson's pocket or his face unlocking it.

## Why it works

The proof comes from the physical phone you paired with, so how someone sounds doesn't matter. Each check is brand new and expires in seconds, so a scammer can't record an old answer and play it back. Even if someone broke into our servers, they still couldn't produce a green check, because the secret never leaves your family's phones.

## The demo

**Cast:** "Grandma" (a judge or volunteer), "Alex" (a teammate playing the grandson), and "the Scammer" (another teammate).

**Scene 1: Setup (20 seconds)**
Grandma and Alex hold their phones together. Both screens say "Paired with each other." Done.

**Scene 2: The scam call (45 seconds)**
Grandma's phone rings. A voice that sounds exactly like Alex says: *"Grandma, I got in a car accident, I need $2,000 for bail, please don't tell Mom."* We play a real AI voice clone so the audience hears how convincing it is.

Grandma taps **Verify**. The Scammer has no way to answer. After a few seconds her screen turns **red**: *"Could not verify Alex. Do not send money."*

**Scene 3: The real call (30 seconds)**
Now the real Alex calls. Grandma taps **Verify**. Alex's phone buzzes: *"Grandma is checking it's you."* He looks at his phone, Face ID confirms him, and Grandma's screen turns **green**: *"Verified: this is Alex."*

**Scene 4: The clever attacker (30 seconds)**
The Scammer tries to replay Alex's earlier green answer. Kinship rejects it instantly because every check is one-time only. Red again.

**Closing line:**
*"Voices can be faked. Your family's phones can't. Kinship gives every family a one-tap answer to 'is this really you?'"*
